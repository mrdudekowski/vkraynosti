# SEO V2 — 90-day roadmap

## Days 0–30: protect and measure

- Connect and verify Google Search Console and Yandex Webmaster.
- Export baseline queries, pages, exclusions, crawl errors, CTR and CWV.
- Keep daily deploy smoke checks for sitemap, canonical, 404 and rendered tour content.
- Audit CMS → snapshot → SSG/OG/sitemap source-of-truth on one representative active and one inactive tour.
- Review all zero-future-date pages with the catalogue owner; choose a consistent temporary-date message.

## Days 31–60: improve qualified landing pages

- Prioritize 5–10 pages using impressions, CTR and business margin rather than generic keyword volume.
- Rewrite titles/descriptions and first-screen copy from verified route facts and search intent.
- Strengthen season ↔ tour internal links and breadcrumbs.
- Improve image delivery and measure LCP/INP/CLS in field data before tuning.
- Validate organization/contact/review/local entity signals and keep NAP consistent.

## Days 61–90: scale what worked

- Create only distinct content gaps confirmed by query data.
- Compare before/after CTR, indexed pages, qualified leads and route-level conversion.
- Formalize monthly publishing/archive review and ownership.
- Add monitoring for stale dates, unexpected noindex, sitemap drift and soft-404 responses.
- Revisit structured data only where the business fields are complete and policy-valid.

## Success criteria

- No P0 crawlability regressions.
- 100% of sitemap URLs pass HTTP/canonical/noindex smoke checks.
- Every indexed tour has a clear intent, current route facts and an owner.
- Decisions are tied to GSC/Yandex/CWV evidence, not arbitrary metadata lengths.
